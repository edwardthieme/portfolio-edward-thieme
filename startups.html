Startup 01

Lya Health

Transform your healthcare appointments with the power of AI. Summarise consultations efficiently, obtain powerful insights by structuring information in real time, and increase your consultation capacity by up to 40%.

The Product

An AI Clinical
Assistant

Lya Health is designed for doctors — giving them an AI-powered co-pilot during every patient consultation. The app listens, structures, and surfaces insights in real time, freeing doctors from the keyboard so they can focus entirely on the patient.

Doctors can choose from speciality-specific assistants — general practitioner, paediatrics, cardiology — each pre-configured with the relevant clinical cards and logic.

+40%
Increase in consultation capacity — doctors see more patients in the same time
  • ◈
    Real-Time Transcription & Summarisation
    Automatically transcribes and structures the patient's narrative during the consultation.
  • ◈
    Diagnostic Hypothesis Generation
    Surfaces possible diagnoses based on what the patient is describing, cross-referenced with medical history.
  • ◈
    Multidimensional Sentiment Analysis
    Analyses anxiety, depression, stress, and pain levels — surfaced visually for the doctor.
  • ◈
    Question Suggestions
    Suggests relevant follow-up questions based on the patient's evolving narrative.
  • ◈
    PubMed Research Integration
    Doctors can search clinical literature directly within the consultation interface.

Startup 02

Nina Pet Health

Your pet's health starts here. A comprehensive pet healthcare app that connects pet owners with veterinary professionals — for telemedicine, in-clinic scheduling, and personalised care management.

The Product

Pet Healthcare
in Your Pocket

Nina Pet Health is built around the idea that pets deserve the same quality of care management that humans have. The app guides owners through onboarding their pet, connects them to a dedicated care team, and tracks health across the pet's entire life.

The experience is warm, playful, and deeply practical — combining the emotional bond owners have with their pets with the clinical rigour needed to keep them healthy.

  • ◈
    Telemedicine (Online Vet)
    Connect with a veterinarian instantly or schedule a consultation — from anywhere.
  • ◈
    In-Clinic Scheduling
    Book physical appointments, exams, and surgeries with full pre-care instructions.
  • ◈
    Personalised Care Plans
    AI-driven care recommendations based on the pet's profile, breed, and health history.
  • ◈
    Onboarding by Pet Profile
    Personalised onboarding questions capture the pet's sex, breed, age, and health conditions.
  • ◈
    Care & Prescription Tracking
    Track medications, treatments, and vet-prescribed care routines over time.
