Discovery

Understanding
the Problem

We conducted research interviews with XP stakeholders, advisors, and customers to understand the real pain points.

Our goal was to help advisors shift their focus from administrative tasks toward building stronger client relationships — while minimising time spent on back-office processes.

Research spanned multiple states and office types, covering the full diversity of the XP advisor network.

The research revealed that advisors spent the majority of their time on manual processes, spreadsheets, and back-office tasks — leaving little room for actual relationship building with clients.

Design & Delivery

An Integrated
Ecosystem

We established an integrated ecosystem of digital products designed for financial advisors — the first financial advisor app in Latin America. Connected to CRM and back-office systems to give advisors access to client portfolios from any location.

Feature 01

Daily Summary

Gives advisors full control from activity cards and consolidated information about their customer portfolio. Pending tasks, overdue items, and upcoming meetings — all in one glance.

Feature 02 & 03

Customer Wallet, Portfolio & Shopping Cart

Get consolidated data from your customer portfolio, including withdrawals, NPS, and NET. The full investment picture in one place.

Seamlessly select from a variety of investment opportunities and efficiently manage orders for multiple clients directly within the app.

−90%

Feature 04 & 05

Organise Your Schedule & Smart Push

An exclusive calendar seamlessly integrated with CRM — easily manages all appointments, client and business meetings, and financial requirements.

Receive customer transaction notifications with automated investment suggestions. Once accepted, these are processed automatically — turning a notification into a completed trade.
